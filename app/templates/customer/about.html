ABOUT SPAREPRO

Online Spare Parts Ordering System for Motors and Pumps

SparePro is a dedicated industrial e-commerce platform built to streamline the sourcing, stock verification, and ordering of high-performance electric motor and pump spare parts.

What SparePro Provides

SparePro bridges the gap between industrial maintenance workshops, repair technicians, plant engineers, and genuine spare part manufacturers. We eliminate component ambiguity by publishing precise technical specifications, shaft dimensions, frame sizes, and model cross-references for every catalog item.

Motor Spare Parts

Comprehensive stock of rotor shaft bearings (6203, 6204, 6205, 6306), motor cooling fans, terminal boards, junction box covers, centrifugal switches, and motor run/start capacitors (36 MFD to 150 MFD).

Pump Spare Parts

Heavy-duty mechanical shaft seals (carbon vs ceramic, silicon carbide), cast bronze and stainless steel impellers, precision SS-304/SS-316 drive shafts, EPDM flange gaskets, and Lovejoy flexible jaw couplings.

Streamlined Ordering & Stock Availability

With real-time database synchronization, users can instantly verify stock quantities, search by exact SKU or brand part numbers, compare technical parameters, and place orders with automated status updates.
